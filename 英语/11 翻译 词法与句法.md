# 翻译 词法与句法

> [!abstract]
> 词法翻译六技（专有名词 / 代词指代 / 一词多义 / 词性转换 / 增词减词 / 说人话）与句法翻译三从句（名词性从句 / 定语从句 / 状语从句），全部配真题例句。

## 11.1 专有名词

**概念**：指特定的、独一无二的人或物（人名、地名、国家名、景观名、单位名、作品名等），通常第一个字母大写。

**翻译方法**

1. 常识类专有名词：按约定俗成的翻译方法翻译
2. 非常识类专有名词：用发音相近的汉字翻译（音译法）
3. 不译，保持不变

**真题讲解**

- 人名：Ted Ning（2010）、James Herriot（2019）、Bill Gates（2018）、William Wordsworth（2023）
- 地名：Chicago（2021）、Maine（2015）、Georgian（2015）、Silicon Valley（2012）、West Germany（2021）
- 作品名：Hair（2013）
- 专业名：Fashion Media & Promotion（2017）
- 机构名：Food Marketing Institute（2016）

## 11.2 代词指代

**翻译方法**

1. 直接译为代词——无歧义时直接译为代词
2. 找出具体指代的名词还原——若直译为代词有歧义

**代词指代原则**

1. **就近原则**：代词一般位于所指代内容之后，由近及远在上文寻找答案
2. **语法及语义一致原则**：个别代词与所指内容相距较远，根据语法及语义判断

> [!example] 真题 2012（人才流失）
> The "brain drain" has long bothered policymakers in poor countries. **They** fear that it hurts their economies, depriving them of much-needed skilled workers who could have taught at their universities, worked in their hospitals and come up with clever new products for their factories to make.

> [!example] 真题 2015（熟路效应）
> When we travel down a well-known route, because we don't have to concentrate much, time seems to flow more quickly. And afterwards, when we come to think back on **it**, we can't remember the journey well because we didn't pay much attention to **it**. So we assume **it** was shorter.

## 11.3 一词多义

**本质**：词无本义，义由境生

**分类**

1. 词性不同，词义不同
2. 词性相同，语境不同，词义不同

> [!example] 真题 2001
> There will be television chat shows hosted by robots, and cars with pollution monitors that will disable them when they offend.

> [!example] 真题 2011
> However, Google and other big tech providers monitor their efficiency closely and make improvements. Monitoring is the first step on the road to reduction, but there is much to be done, and not just by big companies.

> [!example] 真题 2016
> The average supermarket, according to the Food Marketing Institute, carries some 44,000 different items, and many carry tens of thousands more. The sheer volume of available choice is enough to send shoppers into a state of information overload.

## 11.4 词性转换

**含义**：改变词性以求译文通顺自然，合乎汉语表达习惯

**英汉差异**：英语是静态语言（名词/介词/形容词用得多），汉语是动态语言（多用动词）→ 英：名词/介词/形容词 → 汉：动词

> [!example] 真题 2016
> After about 40 minutes of shopping, most people stop struggling to be rationally selective, and instead begin shopping emotionally.

> [!example] 真题 2021
> Researchers have also recently found that interacting with strangers actually brings a boost in mood and feelings of belonging that we didn't expect.

## 11.5 增词减词

**含义**：增词减词以求译文通顺自然，合乎汉语表达习惯

**英汉差异**：英语忌重复、多省略；汉语多重复

**增词法**

1. 虽无其形，但有其义
2. 把英语省译内容增译出来
3. 增加表示数量的词
4. 抽象含义，增译使之具体
5. 增加逻辑词
6. 增加表示时态的助词

**省略法**

1. 累赘、重复内容需删除
2. 翻译出来啰嗦、多余内容需删除

> [!example] 真题 2011
> A Google search can leak between 0.2 and 7.0 grams of CO2, depending on how many attempts are needed to get the "right" answer.

> [!example] 真题 2012
> A big survey of Indian households in 2004 found that nearly 40% of emigrants had more than a high-school education, compared with around 3.3% of all Indians over the age of 25.

> [!example] 真题 2017
> However, during that course I realized that I was not good enough in this area to compete with other creative personalities in the future, so I decided that it was not the right path for me.

## 11.6 说人话

把英文「说人话」——译成符合汉语习惯的地道表达，而不是生硬直译。

> [!example] 真题 2014
> When he feels down—say, after giving a bad lecture—he grants himself permission to be human. → 他会宽慰自己「人非圣贤，孰能无过」。

> [!example] 真题 2015
> Whichever it is, you know every twist and turn like the back of your hand. → 你对每一处转弯都了如指掌。

> [!example] 真题 2018
> Nowadays, his reading material has changed from science fiction and reference books: recently, he revealed that he reads at least 50 nonfiction books a year.

## 11.7 名词性从句

主语从句 / 宾语从句 / 表语从句 / 同位语从句

**翻译原则：基本顺译（按英语原文顺序）**

### 主语从句

> [!example] 真题 2005
> Whether such a sense of fairness evolved independently in capuchins and humans, or whether it stems from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago, is, as yet, an unanswered question.

**it 形式主语的翻译技巧**：英汉差异——汉语先说内容再说评论，英语先说评论再说内容。

1. 顺译，it 不译，译成「……的是」
2. 先译 that 从句，再译 that 前的内容（it 可译可不译）
3. 约定俗成：it is said that...（据说）/ it is reported that...（据报道）/ it is estimated that...（据估计）/ it must be admitted that...（必须承认）等

> [!example] 例句（it 形式主语 + 同位语从句）
> It is true that CEO pay has gone up — top ones may make 300 times the pay of typical workers on average...
> **译文**：诚然，首席执行官（CEO）的薪酬已大幅上涨——高管的平均收入可达普通员工的 300 倍……
> （断句：`It is true that` = 诚然；破折号后为解释补充，顺译即可。）

### 宾语从句

> [!example] 真题 2019
> He had such a pleasant, readable style that one might think that anyone could imitate it.

> [!example] 真题 2017
> However, during that course I realized that I was not good enough in this area to compete with other creative personalities in the future, so I decided that it was not the right path for me.

**it 形式宾语的翻译技巧**

1. 顺译，it 不译
2. 先译真正的宾语（从句 / to do）（it 可译可不译）

> [!example] 例句
> We all find it important that we should make a quick decision about this matter.
> That kind of activity makes it less likely that the court's decisions will be accepted as impartial judgments.

### 表语从句

> [!example] 真题 2015
> The consequence is that you perceive that the trip has taken less time than it actually has.

> [!example] 真题 2017
> This is when I noticed the course "Fashion Media & Promotion".

### 同位语从句

**常见抽象名词**：belief / fact / hope / idea / news / rumor / conclusion / evidence / suggestion / problem / order / answer / decision / discovery / explanation / information / knowledge / promise / opinion / truth 等

**翻译技巧**

1. 前置（类似定语）
2. 译为：即 / ： / -

> [!example] 真题 2007
> Steelworkers, airline employees, and now those in the auto industry are joining millions of families who must worry about interest rates, stock market fluctuation, and the harsh reality that they may outlive their retirement money.

> [!example] 真题 2011
> They found that after a surprise departure, the probability that the company will subsequently have to restate earnings increases by nearly 20%.

## 11.8 定语从句（形容词性从句）

**1. 前置法**：把定语从句翻译到所修饰名词之前，用「的」连接

> [!example] 真题 2017
> So I decided to look for some fashion-related courses that included writing.

**2. 后置法**：把定语从句翻译到所修饰名词之后，处理为主句的并列句

注意：① 重复先行词 ② 用代词代替先行词 ③ 先行词不译出

> [!example] 真题 2011
> While producing large quantities of CO2, these computers emit a great deal of heat, so the centres need to be well air-conditioned, which uses even more energy.
> **译文**：这些计算机在产生大量二氧化碳的同时还散发大量热量，因此数据中心需要配备良好的空调系统，而这又要消耗更多的能量。
> （断句：`which` 指代前文「用空调」整件事 → 后置法译成「而这……」。）

**3. 状译法**：根据主句与从句的逻辑关系，翻译成状语从句

> [!example] 真题 2016
> After about 40 minutes of shopping, most people stop struggling to be rationally selective, and instead begin shopping emotionally – which is the point at which we accumulate the 50 percent of stuff in our cart that we never intended buying.
> **译文**：购物约 40 分钟后，大多数人不再费心做理性选择，而是开始凭感觉购物——购物车里大约一半「从未打算买」的东西正是在这个时候累积起来的。
> （断句：`which is the point at which...` = 「正是在这个时候」，时间状译；`that we never intended buying` 前置于 `stuff` 之内化处理。）

## 11.9 状语从句（副词性从句）

**翻译技巧：前置**——英语状语从句可在主句之前或之后，汉语习惯在主句之前。

> [!example] 真题 2013
> When I think of a sad memory, I do what everybody does—try to put it to one side.

> [!example] 真题 2015
> And afterwards, when we come to think back on it, we can't remember the journey well because we didn't pay much attention to it.
