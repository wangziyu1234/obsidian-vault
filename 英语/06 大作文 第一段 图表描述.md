# 大作文 第一段 图表描述

> [!abstract]
> 第一段 3–4 句话：**图表主题（1 句）+ 图表描述（2 句）+ 图表总结（1 句，非必要）**。动态图描述趋势（一/二/三变量），静态图描述占比（罗列法/重点对比）。

## 6.1 总体结构

图表主题（1 句）→ 图表描述（2 句）→ 图表总结（1 句，非必要）

**图表类型词汇**

| 中文 | 英文 |
| :-- | :-- |
| 柱状图 | bar chart |
| 饼状图 | pie chart |
| 折线图 | line chart |
| 表格 | table |

## 6.2 第 1 句：图表主题

**模板句 1**：该图表提供/显示/反映有关...的情况/信息，根据这个图表可以看到...

```text
The chart above shows/provides/manifests the situation/information about
the change of / factors of ... According to it, ... / It is clear that ... / It can be seen that ...
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2016
> The chart above provides information about an investigation into the purposes of traveling among students in a particular college. According to it, ....

**模板句 2**：该图表对...和...之间在...方面进行了具体的比较，正如图表里生动/清晰/准确地揭示出来的...

```text
The chart above makes a specific comparison between ... and ... / among ... in terms of ...
As is vividly/distinctly/accurately revealed in it, ...
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2010
> The chart above makes a specific comparison between developing and developed countries in terms of mobile-phone subscriptions. As is vividly/distinctly/accurately revealed in it, ...

> [!note] 实战练习
> - 2011：2008 年、2009 年国内轿车市场部分品牌市场份额
> - 2014：20 年间中国城镇人口与乡村人口变化

## 6.3 第 2–3 句：图表描述（动态图 · 一个变量）

**整体趋势 + 起点终点 + 重要节点**

```text
变量, showing an upward/downward trend from 时间 to 时间, rises/falls (sharply/steadily).
To be specific, it rises/falls from 百分比/数字 in 时间 to 百分比/数字 in 时间,
and then increases/surges/mounts/drops (steadily/sharply) to 百分比/数字 in 时间.
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2013
> The number of college students taking part-time jobs, showing an upward trend from the freshman year to the senior year, rises steadily. To be specific, it rises from 67.77% in the freshman year to 71.93% in the junior year, and then surges to 88.24% in the senior year.

> [!note] 实战练习
> - 2023：2012–2021 健康素养（health literacy）

## 6.4 第 2–3 句：图表描述（动态图 · 两个变量）

**连接词**

- 表示相同变化趋势：meanwhile, likewise, similarly
- 表示不同变化趋势：however, yet, while, ironically, on the contrary, in contrast, by contrast, conversely

```text
变量A rises/falls (sharply/steadily) from 百分比/数字 in 时间 to 百分比/数字 in 时间,
and then mounts/drops (steadily/sharply) to 百分比/数字 in 时间.
Likewise/In contrast, 变量B increases/decreases (sharply/steadily/slowly),
from 百分比/数字 to 百分比/数字 during the same period.
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2010
> The number of mobile phone subscribers in developing countries rose steadily from less than 0.5 billion in 2000 to about 0.9 billion in 2003, and then surged to 4 billion in 2008. In contrast, the number of subscribers in developed countries increased rather slowly, from about 0.6 billion to 1 billion during the same period.

## 6.5 第 2–3 句：图表描述（动态图 · 三个变量）

```text
The number of 变量A increased dramatically from 百分比/数字 in 时间 to 百分比/数字 in 时间,
while that of B decreased significantly from ____ to ____ during the same period.
Meanwhile/On the contrary, there was a/an (slight/obvious) decline/growth in that of C.
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2011
> The car market share of Chinese brands increased dramatically from 25% in 2008 to about 33% in 2009, while that of Japanese brands decreased significantly from around 34% to some 26% during the same period. Meanwhile, there was a slight decline in that of American brands.

## 6.6 第 2–3 句：图表描述（静态图 · 变量较少：罗列法）

```text
A, B, C, and D account for ____, ____, ____ and ____ respectively.
Among them, it can be seen that the highest/lowest is D, which is/takes up ____.
```

进阶版（突出最高项与倍数关系）：

```text
Among them, it can be seen that ____ vastly outreaches other items.
Meanwhile, we can see that the highest/lowest is D, which is approximately ____ times higher than others.
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2015
> Spending on new year gifts, transportation, dinner parties and other activities account for 40%, 20%, 20% and 20% respectively. Among them, it can be seen that the highest is spending on New Year gifts, which takes up 40%.

## 6.7 第 2–3 句：图表描述（静态图 · 变量较多：重点数据对比）

**模板句 1**：A 和 B（被证明是主导因素）分别占...，相反 C 和 D 仅占...

```text
A, and B, (proven to be dominant factors), account for ____ and ____ respectively.
In contrast, C and D only make up ____ and ____.
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2018
> Specialty, service and environment, proven to be dominant factors, account for 36.3%, 27.4% and 24.3% respectively. In contrast, price only makes up 7.3%.

**模板句 2**：A 占比最大为...，其次是 B 和 C 分别占...，相比之下 D 仅占一小部分

```text
It can be seen that A accounts for the largest part, with a proportion of __%,
followed by B and C which take(s) up __% and __% respectively.
In contrast, D only occupies __%.
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2018
> It can be seen that specialty accounts for the largest part, with a proportion of 36.3%, followed by service and environment which take up 27.4% and 24.3% respectively. In contrast, price only occupies 7.3%.

## 6.8 第 4 句：图表总结（非必要）

**模板句 1**：尽管这些数字看似孤立，事实上它们彼此紧密相连

```text
Isolated as the figures seem to be, as a matter of fact, they are closely connected to one another.
```

**模板句 2**：尽管有点抽象，但该图表背后却暗含深意

```text
In spite of its abstract appearance, the chart carries profound implications.
```

**模板句 3**：作者仅仅是想展示给我们这个统计么？当然不是。真正的目的是引导我们深入探索这一现象

```text
Does the author merely intend to present these statistics? Certainly not.
The real purpose is to guide us to explore this phenomenon in depth.
```
