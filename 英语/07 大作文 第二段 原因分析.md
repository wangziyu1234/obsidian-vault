# 大作文 第二段 原因分析

> [!abstract]
> 第二段 3–4 句话：**引出原因（1 句）+ 原因分析（2–3 句）**。原因分析有两种结构——结构 1「原因 1+原因 2+原因 3」，结构 2「原因 1+原因 2+例子」。

## 7.1 总体结构

引出原因（1 句）→ 原因 1 → 原因 2 →（原因 3 / 例子）

**分析角度**

- 原因 1：宏观——社会背景
- 原因 2：微观——精神、物质、效率
- 原因 3：政策引导、媒体

## 7.2 第 1 句：引出原因

**模板句 1**：一些因素可能会严重影响这一现象。但是，最重要的是以下两个方面。

```text
Several factors could account for this phenomenon.
Yet, topping the list are the following two aspects.
```

**模板句 2**：您可能想知道，为什么这种现象在当今的中国如此盛行？原因如下。

```text
Why, you may wonder, is this phenomenon so prominent in China nowadays?
The driving factors for this situation are listed as follows. / The reasons are as follows.
```

**模板句 3**：是什么导致了这种现象？这是一个发人深省的问题。

```text
What exactly contributes to this phenomenon? It is a thought-provoking question.
```

## 7.3 结构 1：原因 1 + 原因 2 +（原因 3）

**模板句 1**（原因 1：宏观社会背景）：第一，随着...，大量的人/毕业生开始...，强调...这一现象。

```text
First/Firstly, with the ____, people/undergraduates in mounting numbers begin to ____,
laying emphasis on sth./doing sth..
```

**模板句 2**（原因 2：微观因素）：第二/另外，...因素也被证明是造成这种现象的原因，因为人们可以通过...，这对他们的行为产生了重大影响。

```text
Second/Besides, ____(factors) also prove(s) responsible for this phenomenon
in that people can ____ through ____, which exerts significant influence on their behavior.
```

**模板句 3**（原因 3：政策/媒体）：第三/最后，政府政策的积极引导/大众传媒潜移默化的影响是另一个不可忽视的关键因素。

```text
Third/Last but not least, the positive guidance of the government policies
/the subtle influence of the mass media (on sth./doing sth.) is another key factor that can't be ignored.
```

**模板句 4**：许多人们开始重视...，主要是因为...

```text
That a majority of people begin to attach importance to ... is mainly due to the fact that ...
```

**模板句 5**（利弊视角）：虽然在某种程度上做某事可能会不好，但从我的角度来看，它非常有价值，因为它将帮助...

```text
Although doing sth., to some extent, may (bring drawbacks),
from my perspective, it proves very worthwhile as it will help ...
```

**模板例句 1**：随着工作节奏以及社会竞争的日益激烈，大量的毕业生开始改变他们的学习观念，重视参与兼职工作。

```text
With the faster pace of work and intensifying social competition,
undergraduates in mounting numbers begin to change their study concepts,
laying emphasis on participating in part-time jobs.
```

**模板例句 2**：经济因素也被证明是造成这种现象的原因，因为人们可以通过购买国产汽车来节省大量金钱，这对他们的行为产生了重大影响。

```text
Financial factors also prove responsible for this phenomenon
in that people can conserve a large amount of money through purchasing domestic cars,
which exerts significant influence on their behavior.
```

**模板例句 3**：最后，政府政策的积极引导/大众传媒潜移默化的影响是另一个不可忽视的关键因素。

```text
Last but not least, the positive guidance of the government policies
/the subtle influence of the mass media is another key factor that can't be ignored.
```

**模板例句 4**：发展中国家许多人们开始重视移动产业的发展，主要是因为它极大地影响了我们的生活方式、学习方式和娱乐方式，尤其是我们获取信息的方式。

```text
That a majority of people in developing countries begin to attach importance
to the development of the mobile industry is mainly due to the fact that
it significantly affects the way we live, learn and play,
especially the way we acquire information.
```

**模板例句 5**：虽然在某种程度上参观博物馆可能会花费一些闲暇时间/多花一些钱，但从我的角度来看，它是非常有价值的，因为它将帮助孩子们加深对历史的理解。

```text
Although visiting museums, to some extent, may cost some leisure time/a little more money,
it, from my perspective, proves very worthwhile (detrimental) as it will help children
to (hinder people from doing sth.) deepen their understanding of history.
```

## 7.4 结构 2：原因 1 + 原因 2 + 例子

**引出例子模板句**

1. 在我所见、所闻、所经历的所有和这一问题相关的难忘例子中，最有说服力的是下面这个。

```text
Among all the memorable examples related to this topic which I have ever observed,
heard and experienced, the following one is the most persuasive.
```

2. 关于这个问题，我找不到比下面这个更令人信服的例子了。

```text
Regarding this issue, I can find no more convincing example than the following one.
```

3. 关于这个问题，可以给出很多有说服力的例子，但下面这个就足够了。

```text
Concerning this issue, numerous instances can be provided, but the following one suffices.
```

**例子叙述 · 思路 1：叙事型**（主体+动作，产生某种结果或影响）

> [!example] 例句
> Last Spring Festival, I went back to my hometown to visit my relatives and bring them some gifts. To my great surprise, I found these gifts were more than three times as expensive as before.

**例子叙述 · 思路 2：对比型**（某人过去...，之后做了某事，现在...）

> [!example] 例句
> My uncle, who lives in a remote western village of our country, never heard of mobile-phones fifteen years ago. In recent years, however, the living standards of his village have been greatly improved thanks to booming agriculture. Almost everyone owns mobile-phones.

**例子叙述 · 思路 3：模板型**（典型人物 + 同位语 + 强调句）

```text
At this very moment, a typical example pops into my mind—one of my friends
who always worked as a tutor during college.
It is the working experience that he gained that helped him land a job in a private school.
```
