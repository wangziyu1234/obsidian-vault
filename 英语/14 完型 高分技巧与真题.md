## 14.2 B. 利用逻辑关系解题

### 顺接关系

**并列词**：and, and also, or, either...or..., neither...nor..., not only...but also..., likewise, similarly, equally, in the same way, that is (to say), as well as, much the same as; on the one hand...on the other hand; for one thing...for another; one...another; some...others...still others...

**递进词**：also, then, besides, additionally, in addition, furthermore, moreover, what's more, indeed, even, in effect, in fact

### 相反关系

**转折词**：but, however, yet, contrarily, on the contrary, by contrast, unfortunately, in fact, in reality, actually, on the other hand

**让步词**：although, though, even though, even if, nevertheless, despite, in spite of, regardless of, anyway, anyhow, even

**对比词**：while, whereas, rather than..., instead of, not...but...

### 因果关系

**因果词**：because, for, since, as, thus, hence, therefore, so, so...that..., such...that..., consequently, accordingly, due to, thanks to, on account of, account for, considering, seeing that, in that, now that, as a result, for this reason

**目的词**：in order that..., for, in order to, so that, lest, for fear that, in case

**条件词**：if, only if, unless, otherwise, as soon as, as long as, in case, suppose that, supposing that, provided that, providing that, with (the help of), when, whenever

### 举例关系

such as, for example, for instance, of (these, those, them), among (these, those, them), to illustrate, as an illustration, to take an example, more specifically speaking, namely

### 时间关系

1. 顺序递进/先后罗列：first, second, last of all; in the first place, in the second place, finally; to begin with, to continue; first...then...
2. 时间点：when, whenever, before, after, since, as, until, till, simultaneously, meanwhile, in the meantime, at the same time
