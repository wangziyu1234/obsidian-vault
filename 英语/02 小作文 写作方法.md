# 小作文 写作方法

> [!abstract]
> 三段式框架：**第一段 2 句**（写信缘由 + 寒暄/自我介绍 + 写信目的）、**第二段 4 句**（总起句 + 分层阐述）、**第三段 2 句**（致谢/歉意 + 期待回信 + 祝福）。附七类信件备用词汇。

## 2.1 第一段（2 句话）：写信缘由 + 寒暄/自我介绍 + 写信目的

**写信缘由（非必须）**

- I'm glad that... / I'm so delighted that... —— 我很高兴...
- I'm glad to receive your letter/email asking me... —— 我很高兴收到你询问我...的信
- It is a great honor to inform you that... / I'm honored to do sth. —— 我很荣幸...
- I heard that... —— 我听说...
- Thank you for doing sth. —— 感谢你...

**寒暄（私人信件）**

- Long time no see. —— 好久不见
- How is everything going recently? —— 近来可好？
- I have arrived home safe and sound. —— 我已平安到家。（感谢信/道歉信）
- Haven't heard from you for long time. —— 很久没有收到你的消息了。
- I hope this letter finds you well. —— 我希望这封信抵达时，你一切安好。

**自我介绍**

- I am an undergraduate in ... university. —— 我是...大学的本科生
- I am an employee/staff member of ... company. —— 我是...公司的职员
- I am the customer who had supper in your restaurant yesterday. —— 我是昨天在你店里吃晚餐的顾客。

**写信目的**

- I am writing to... / I am writing this letter/email to... —— 我写这封信的目的是...
- I, as the chairman of the Students' Union, am planning... —— 作为学生会主席，我打算...
- I, on behalf of the Students' Union, am writing (this letter) for the purpose of doing... —— 我代表学生会写这封信的目的是...

## 2.2 第二段（4 句话）：总起句 + 分层阐述

**总起句**

- Details are as follows. —— 相关详细信息如下。
- Provided below are my personal qualifications. —— 以下展示的是我的个人条件。
- My decision can be explained by the following factors. —— 以下原因可以解释我的决定。
- The following factors can account for my decision. —— 以下原因可以解释我的决定。

**分层阐述（三种思路）**

1. 思路 1：指令已给出，按指令写
2. 思路 2：原因 1，原因 2，原因 3
3. 思路 3：What……；Why……；How……

**逻辑衔接词**

- Firstly,... Secondly,... Finally,...
- To begin with,... Besides,... Moreover/Furthermore/In addition,...

## 2.3 第三段（2 句话）：致谢/歉意 + 联系方式 + 期待回信 + 祝福

**建议信/邀请信/投诉信/推荐信 —— 表达感谢/歉意/希望**

- I would be deeply grateful if you... —— 如果您...我将不胜感激
- We would be pleased if you could accept my... —— 我(们)很高兴，如果你能采纳我的建议/接受我的邀请/尽快处理投诉
- I hope you are interested in this activity. —— 我希望你能对该活动感兴趣。

**希望建议有用**

- I hope my suggestion/the information above will be helpful to you. / I hope you find my advice helpful and useful. —— 我希望以上建议/信息对你有帮助。

**联系方式**

- If you are interested in this activity, please contact us no later than this Friday. —— 如果你对本次活动感兴趣，请于本周五前联系我们。
- If you are interested in this activity, please call 12345 or send your application to 123@qq.com. —— 若你对本次活动感兴趣，可致电 12345，或将申请材料发送至邮箱 123@qq.com。
- Feel free to contact me if you have any further questions. —— 如有其他问题，欢迎随时联系我。

**感谢信/道歉信/祝贺信**

- Again, I would like to express my heartfelt... to you! Please accept my... —— 我将再次表达我对你的感谢/道歉/祝贺
- I feel grateful to you beyond words. —— 我对您的感激难以言表。
- I would like to express my sincere appreciation/apology to you once more at the conclusion of this letter. —— 我想在结尾处再次向您表示诚挚感谢/歉意。
- I am indeed sorry for my mistake/carelessness, and I would be deeply grateful if I can get your understanding and forgiveness. —— 对于自己的过失/粗心，我确实深表歉意，若能得到您的理解和原谅，我将不胜感激。

**期待回信**

- I'm looking forward to your reply. —— 静候佳音
- I'm looking forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience. —— 期待你的回复
- I am looking forward to your participation/joining. —— 期待你的加入（邀请信/通知）

**祝福语**

- Enjoy yourself on Sunday. / Have fun! —— 玩的开心！
- Wish you a wonderful college life. —— 祝你大学生活精彩纷呈！
- Wish you all the best. —— 万事如意！

## 2.4 附录（备用词汇）

| 信件 | 动词 | 名词 |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 建议信 | advise sb. to do sth. / suggest doing sth. | make/give some suggestions/advice/proposals to sb. |
| 邀请信 | invite sb. to do sth. | send sb. an invitation to do sth. |
| 感谢信 | thank you for (doing) sth. | express my sincere thanks/gratitude to you for (doing) sth. |
| 道歉信 | apologize for (doing) sth. | make an apology for (doing) sth. |
| 推荐信 | recommend sb. sth. / recommend sth. to sb. / recommend doing sth. / recommend that sb. (should) do sth. | make a recommendation to sb. |
| 祝贺信 | congratulate sb. on/upon sth. | express/convey my congratulations to sb. on sth. |
| 投诉信 | complain to sb. about sth. | make a complaint about sth. |
| 通知/告示 | — | announcement / notice |
