# 小作文 九类模板

> [!abstract]
> 九类小作文完整模板，其中建议信含个人版与大众倡议版两种变体，共 10 套。分类与句型见 [[03 小作文 分类与句型]]；空白处（____）按题目填写，括号内为可替换内容。

## 4.1 模板 1：建议信（个人）

```text
Dear _____,

I am ____. I am writing to give you some suggestions regarding sth.
In my humble opinion, you would be wise to take the following actions.
In the first place, 建议一. What is more, 建议二. Finally, 建议三.
I hope you will find these proposals useful, and I would be ready
to discuss this matter with you for further details. Good luck with your life and work.

（结尾二选一）或 I will be grateful if you could take my suggestions seriously.
I hope you will find them helpful and practical. I'm looking forward to your early reply.

Yours Sincerely,
Li Ming
```

## 4.2 模板 1'：倡议信（大众）

```text
Since some of you may have heard that _____(具体事情).
I am writing, on behalf of _____(组织) and in my own name, to appeal for ______(呼吁内容).
The relevant suggestions are provided as follows.
In the first place, 建议一. What is more, 建议二. Finally, 建议三.
Hopefully you will join us and offer your love and contribution.
Your participation will be highly valued.
Please contact me via 12345@sina.com when you have made a decision.

Truly yours,
Li Ming
```

## 4.3 模板 2：邀请信

```text
Dear ____,

I am ____. It is my great pleasure to invite you to visit/attend ____ and give a lecture.
There will be a 活动 related to 主题 this Sunday（时间可换）in the Student's Center（地点可换）.
As one of the most privileged experts in this field, your attendance will give us unparalleled honor.
I indeed hope that you can come to enjoy ____ and offer your valuable advice.
I am looking forward to your participation.
Please let me know if you can come and send me your reply before 截止时间.

Yours Sincerely,
Li Ming
```

## 4.4 模板 3：感谢信

```text
Dear _____,

I am ____. I am writing this letter to extend my sincere gratitude for ____(感谢内容).
To begin with, it was your generous help and warm company that enabled me to do sth.
and I will cherish the goodwill you rendered to me wherever I go.
What's more, thanks to your kind favor, it is possible for me to do sth. soon enough,
which will be deeply imprinted in my mind all my life.
Last but not least, my hometown, XX, is a fantastic place boasting attractive scenery.
Please come to visit it at your earliest convenience so that I can return your favor.
At the end of this letter, I would like to thank you again for your gracious favor.
I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours Sincerely,
Li Ming
```

## 4.5 模板 4：道歉信

```text
Dear _____,

I am ____. I am writing this letter to express my sincere apology to you.
The details are provided as follows.
To begin with, _______(交代缘由).
Unfortunately, _______(后果).
I know my carelessness has brought you great inconvenience.
To solve this problem, I would like to do sth. to make up for my mistake. (弥补措施)
Please accept my apology and thank you for your understanding.
Feel free to contact me if you have any further questions.

Yours Sincerely,
Li Ming
```

## 4.6 模板 5：推荐/介绍信

```text
Dear _____,

I am ____. It is my great privilege to recommend ______ as the right choice for your consideration.
The primary reasons for this recommendation are as follows.
Firstly, it is one of the most fantastic books/films/tourist attractions
which have attracted millions of readers/viewers/travelers.
(推荐人时换用：she/he is one of the most competent and visionary persons I have ever seen.)
Secondly, _____. Thirdly, _______.
I do hope the above information will help you to understand _____(推荐的人或物) more clearly.
Please feel free to contact me if you have any further questions.

Yours Sincerely,
Li Ming
```

## 4.7 模板 6：祝贺信

```text
Dear _____,

Knowing the information that you have _______(祝贺事件),
I feel so pleased to write this letter to extend my warmest congratulations to you on this special day.
I'm sure that the final realization of your long-cherished dream should be attributed to your enormous devotion.
What distinguished you from others was your effort, which paid off eventually.
To help you achieve greater success, I advise you to maintain
your ambition and courage to face challenges in the future.
Ambition is a magic source of power which can supply lasting confidence
and inspiration to help achieve your goals.
Please accept my congratulations again.
Best wishes for your even greater progress in the future.

Yours Sincerely,
Li Ming
```

## 4.8 模板 7：投诉信

```text
Dear _____,

I am ____. I am writing this letter to express my dissatisfaction about _____.
The problems I have found are as follows.
To begin with, the services/product you offered/delivered are/is far below my expectations,
which gives much inconvenience and trouble to me.
What is more, I found that you had made a terrible mistake about sth.
I would appreciate it very much if you could do something necessary to change this situation,
say, (substitute another qualified one for this or allow us to refund.)
I would be grateful if my advice is adopted and this problem is solved properly.
I'm looking forward to your reply.

Yours Sincerely,
Li Ming
```

## 4.9 模板 8：告示/通知

```text
Notice

_______（活动）will be held in _______（活动地点）on _______(活动日期).
The relevant procedures and arrangement of this activity are announced as follows.
Firstly, this activity will focus on ____(主题).
Moreover, ____(某人) will give an address at the beginning.
Last but not least, all the participants and audience are required to _____(出席要求) during the whole process.
We sincerely welcome you to attend this event at your convenience.
Please contact us no later than _____（时间）.

The Students' Union
December 20th, 2025
```

## 4.10 模板 9：（会议）纪要

```text
Meeting minutes
Subject: the organization of The Food Fair
Location: School Auditorium
Date: December 1st 2023
Time: 10:00 a.m. - 12:00 noon
Attendees: Professor Smith, Mr. Smith - President
Prepared by: Li Ming

Agenda and Notes:
1. It aims to... / sb. reviewed ... and he explained...
2. It includes... / sb. came out with a proposal for sth.
   and his suggestions were listed down in the PowerPoint for everyone's reference.
3. The food fair is to be held... / It was decided to...
```
