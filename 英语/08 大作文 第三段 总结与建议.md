# 大作文 第三段 总结与建议

> [!abstract]
> 第三段两种结构：**结构 1 一句话收尾**（按话题正/负/中立选趋势预测 / 解决措施 / 科学面对）；**结构 2 三步收尾**（论述总结 1 句 + 提出建议 1–2 句 + 憧憬未来 1 句）。

## 8.1 结构 1：趋势/现象预测 / 解决措施 / 科学面对（1 句）

**（1）趋势/现象预测（正面话题）**：基于上述数据和分析，有理由预测该趋势正常且积极，未来几年将继续发展。

```text
Based on the statistics and analysis above, we may reasonably predict
that the trend/tendency/phenomenon is normal and positive;
therefore I firmly believe that it is bound to continue in the years to come.
```

**（2）解决措施（负面话题）**：随着问题日益严峻和紧迫，迫切需要出台相应的政策来解决；更重要的是，我们应当呼吁公众提高对...的认识。

```text
At present, with the problem becoming increasingly severe and urgent,
it is imperative that corresponding policies be introduced to solve this problem.
More importantly, we ought to appeal to the public to raise their awareness of sth.
```

**（3）科学面对（中立话题）**：对这种现象采取客观、科学的态度非常必要，我们有必要适当注意某事的利弊。

```text
According to the discussion mentioned above,
taking an objective and scientific attitude toward the phenomenon is quite essential.
It is necessary that we pay due attention to the advantages and disadvantages of sth.
```

## 8.2 结构 2：论述总结（1 句）+ 提出建议（1–2 句）+ 憧憬未来（1 句）

### （1）论述总结

**模板句 1**：一般而言，这种现象是非常重要的/致命的/有益的/有害的，所以我们必须优先考虑这一现象。

```text
Generally speaking, this phenomenon is so vital/fatal/beneficial/harmful
that it is imperative for us to give priority to it.
```

**模板句 2**：简而言之，从这种现象/问题的各个方面来看，我们应该记住，这种情况对我们自身和社会都具有重大意义/是有益的/有害的。

```text
In short, in view of every aspect of this phenomenon/issue, we should bear in mind
that this situation is of great significance/practically beneficial/detrimental
to both ourselves and society.
```

**模板句 3**：一般而言，这种现象，无论是正面还是负面的，都会在未来持续下去。

```text
Generally speaking, this phenomenon, whether positive or negative, will persist in the near future.
```

**模板句 4**：简而言之，这种现象是相当有利的，必须予以支持、提倡和鼓励；/ 这种现象是有害的，必须予以揭露、禁止。

```text
In short, the phenomenon is rather favorable, which must be supported, advocated and encouraged.
/ In short, the phenomenon is quite detrimental, which must be exposed, combated and prohibited.
```

### （2）提出建议

**建议对象**：宏观——国家、政府、媒体；微观——企业、学校、父母、个人

- 建议媒体应努力加强对...的优点/危害的报道。
- 建议学校和家长鼓励青少年.../阻止青少年...
- 有关当局必须致力于制定积极措施，以促进/遏制这种现象。

```text
It is advisable that the press should endeavor to reinforce their coverage of the virtues/hazards of ...
Schools and parents are expected to encourage teenagers to... / discourage teenagers from ...
The authorities concerned must be committed to formulating proactive measures to promote/curb this phenomenon.
```

> [!example] 真题套用 2015
> For one thing, it is advisable that the press or social media should recommend more appealing restaurants so that people have a full range of choices. For another, restaurants are expected to strive to upgrade their decor and surroundings to cater to customers' ever-changing demands.

### （3）憧憬未来

**模板句 1（中立）**：只有这样，我们才能使我们的生活更加多样化、充满活力。

```text
Only in this way can we make our life more diverse and dynamic.
```

**模板句 2（负面）**：通过采取这些及时而有力的措施，我们完全有理由相信，这一问题将在今后的日子里得到适当解决。

```text
With these timely and strong measures taken, we have every reason to believe
that the problem will be properly resolved in the days to come.
```

**模板句 3（负面）**：我们完全有理由相信，在大家的共同努力下，我们可以取得更大的成就，开创美好的未来。

```text
We have every reason to believe that with the concerted efforts of everyone,
we can make more remarkable achievements and open up a better future.
```

**模板句 4**：我深信，如果我们齐心协力，解决这个问题只是时间问题。

```text
I am deeply convinced that if we make concerted efforts,
it will only be a matter of time to settle this problem.
```
