# 2018 年英语二小作文：取消拜访并重新约定时间

> [!abstract] 本讲定位
> 原 PDF 的完整参考范文，保留全部段落与论述，供逐段背诵、默写和核对。明确错误的最小校正见末尾说明。

## 题目任务

给 Smith 教授写邮件：为取消拜访道歉并解释情况，建议以后见面。

> [!note]- 完整题目 Directions
> 47. Directions: Suppose you have to cancel your travel plan and will not be able to visit Professor Smith. Write him an email to
>
> 1) apologize and explain the situation, and
>
> 2) suggest a future meeting.
>
> You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
>
> Do not use your own name. Use “Li Ming” instead.
>
> Do not write your address. (10 points)

## 中文背诵提纲

1. **第 1 段**：说明不能按计划拜访，并为不便道歉。
2. **第 2 段**：解释临时代替生病同学参会，提出另约方便的时间。
3. **第 3 段**：感谢理解，期待回复。

## 完整参考范文

> [!example]+ ✏️ 完整参考范文
> Dear Professor Smith,
>
> I am writing to apologize for not being able to visit you as planned, since I have to cancel my travel plan for some reason. I am terribly sorry for any
> inconvenience this causes you and any changes that it necessitates in your current plans.
>
> I have been looking forward to seeing you since we made the appointment. Unexpectedly, I was asked to attend an important meeting in place of a classmate who
> had suddenly fallen ill. If it is possible, I would like to reschedule our appointment to a time convenient for you.
>
> Thank you for understanding in this matter. Looking forward to your reply.
>
> Sincerely yours,
>
> Li Ming

> [!note]- 中文译文（原 PDF 全文，已修正明确错字或对应数据）
> 亲爱的史密斯教授:
>
> 我因故不得不取消旅行计划，因此不能如约拜访您了，特写信向您致歉。给您带来的不便以及给您的计划带来的变动，我深表歉意。
>
> 约定后我就一直很期待见到您。没想到，我临时被要求替代一名突然生病的同学参加一个重要会议。如果可能的话，我想重新约定一个您方便的时间与您见面。
>
> 感谢您对此事的理解。期待您的回复。
>
> 真诚的，
>
> 李明

## 迁移表达

- **apologize for not being able to …**：为不能……道歉。
- **at a time convenient for you**：在您方便的时间。
- **reschedule our appointment**：重新安排见面时间。
