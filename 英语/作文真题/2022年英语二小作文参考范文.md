# 2022年英语二小作文参考范文：介绍校园美食节并邀请留学生

> [!abstract] 本讲定位
> 收录原 PDF 的完整参考范文与全文中文译文，保留原段落。先按中文提纲回忆，再背诵英文；默写时可折叠范文。

## 题目任务

向本校留学生写邮件：介绍校园美食节，并邀请他们参加。约 100 词，署名 Li Ming。

> [!note]- 完整 Directions（原 PDF）
> Suppose you are planning a campus food festival. Write an e-mail to the international students in your university to 1) introduce the food festival,
> and 2) invite them to participate. You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not use your own name in the email. Use “Li Ming” instead.
> (10 points)

## 中文背诵提纲

1. **第1段**：交代美食节地点、时间；发出诚挚邀请。
2. **第2段**：介绍地方美食、分组烹饪与现场食材；大家伴着音乐逐摊品尝。
3. **第3段**：强调体验不同文化的机会；期待参加。

## 完整参考范文

> [!example]+ ✏️ 完整参考范文
> Dear international students,
>
> There will be a multicultural food festival at the Main Dining Hall from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. on May 27th. You are cordially invited to this occasion.
>
> The event features a variety of local specialties, ranging from traditional homely delicacies to popular street snacks, all elaborately prepared by
> our catering staff. If interested, you are also encouraged to cook your hometown dishes in groups with the ingredients provided on site. This will
> definitely be a highlight of the event. After preparation, all participants can move along the booths, sampling these delicious foods with cheerful
> music.
>
> Don’t miss this golden opportunity to get a taste of different cultures and experience more joy in life! We keenly look forward to your participation.
>
> Yours sincerely,
>
> Li Ming

> [!note]- 全文中文译文（原 PDF 参考译文）
> 亲爱的留学生们:
>
> 5月27日上午10点至下午3点在主食堂将会举办一场多元文化美食节。诚邀你们一起参加。
>
> 本次活动主打各种地方特色美食，有传统家常菜肴，也有人气街头小吃，均由食堂餐饮人员精心准备。此外，如果感兴趣的话，也鼓励你们利用现场提供的食材烹饪自己的家乡菜。这绝对会成为活动的一大亮点。制作完成后，所有参与者可以伴着欢快的音乐，挨个摊位品尝美食。
>
> 不要错过这次尝试各种不同文化、体验更多生活乐趣的绝佳机会!我们热切期盼你们的到来。
>
> 诚挚的，
>
> 李明

## 可迁移表达

- **You are cordially invited to ...**：诚邀你参加……。
- **ranging from ... to ...**：从……到……，种类多样。
- **We keenly look forward to your participation.**：我们热切期待你的参与。
